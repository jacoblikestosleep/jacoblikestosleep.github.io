What makes it different

Most chat apps forget you the moment you close them. The point of this one is that it does not.

It remembers

The interview you were dreading, the friend you fell out with, what you said you would do this week. Come back tomorrow and it is still there.

Twelve companions

Each with their own personality, way of talking and life going on around them. You pick who you want to speak to.

It picks up where you left off

No re-explaining yourself at the start of every conversation, which is the thing that makes most chatbots exhausting.

No account needed to start

Open it and talk. Nothing to sign up for before you find out whether you like it.

Honest about being AI

Myra's companions are AI characters, not real people, and the app says so rather than letting you wonder. It will not claim to be human if you ask.

It is company, not care. If you are struggling with something serious, the app points you at real crisis resources rather than trying to handle it — and those are one tap away, not buried.
