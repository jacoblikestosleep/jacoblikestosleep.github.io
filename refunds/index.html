How you know it is legitimate

This category is full of apps that want your details. Here is what this one will never do.

  • ✓It never files for you. Every claim link opens the official site — a government agency or the court-appointed administrator — in your own browser.
  • ✓It never asks for your Social Security number, your bank details or your claim forms. No legitimate refund-finding app needs them.
  • ✓It never charges to file. Filing is free on the official site, every time, and the app says so on every card.
  • ✓Every card shows when it was last checked. Programs past their deadline leave the feed the same day, automatically.
  • ✓There is no account. No name, no email, no password — there is nothing to breach.

A real refund never requires a payment. If anyone asks you to pay a fee, buy a gift card or send crypto to release a payout, it is a scam. The app has a whole screen about how to tell the difference.
