Bela – hrvatska kartaška igra · com.boonwiz.bela · English

Zadnja izmjena: 7. studenoga 2026.

Objavljuje BoonWiz.

Ukratko

  • Nema računa, prijave ni registracije.
  • Tvoje igre, statistika, bela blok i postavke ostaju na tvom telefonu. Nemamo vlastiti poslužitelj.
  • Uz tvoj pristanak (gdje ga zakon traži) aplikacija šalje statistiku korištenja u Google Analytics za Firebase — npr. da je partija počela ili završila — vezanu uz slučajni identifikator instalacije, nikad uz tvoje ime.
  • Aplikacija ne traži pristup fotografijama, datotekama, kontaktima, kameri ni lokaciji. Dopuštenje za obavijesti traži samo ako sam uključiš podsjetnike.
  • Aplikacija je besplatna jer prikazuje oglase Google AdMoba. Oglase možeš ukloniti jednokratnom kupnjom.

Što ostaje na tvom uređaju

Spremljeno u internoj pohrani aplikacije. Ti se zapisi sami ne šalju nikamo (o statistici korištenja — niže):

  • Postavke. Pravila igre (granica partije, broj partija u meču, regionalna i kućna pravila), razina računala, brzina, izgled karata (mađarice ili francuske, veličina, četiri boje), jezik i podsjetnik.
  • Igra u tijeku. Meč i izazov dana u tijeku (karte, zvanja, odigrani štihovi i rezultat), da ih možeš dovršiti kasnije.
  • Statistika. Odigrani i dobiveni mečevi i partije, dijeljenja, zvanja aduta, padovi, štiglje, zvanja i bele te, za svakog igrača, bodovi koje je dobio u ruku i koliko je često imao dečka u adutu.
  • Bela blok. Dijeljenja koja upišeš.
  • Napredak. Rezultati izazova dana, niz dana, ponovni pokušaji, tjedni izazov, rang, rejting i postignuća.
  • Oglasi i pitanja. Koliko si oglasa preko cijelog zaslona vidio, kad ti je zadnji put ponuđeno „Ukloni reklame”, kad smo te zadnji put zamolili za ocjenu i jesi li odigrao prvu partiju.
  • Podsjetnici. Jesu li uključeni i jesmo li te već pitali želiš li ih, sati tvojih zadnjih nekoliko posjeta (da te podsjetimo oko vremena kad obično igraš), kad si zadnji put podsjećen i koliko podsjetnika zaredom nisi otvorio.
  • Kupnja. Je li kupljeno „Ukloni reklame”.

Pošteno dijeljenje. Ključ iz kojeg se računaju karte svakog dijeljenja izvlači se na tvom uređaju. Njegov kod (otisak SHA-256) prije dijeljenja i sam ključ nakon njega prikazujemo samo tebi, u aplikaciji. Ne šalju se nikamo.

Izazov dana. Karte izazova dana proizlaze iz samog datuma: računa ih tvoj telefon, bez poslužitelja. Rezultat ostaje na uređaju; napušta ga samo ako ga sam podijeliš, putem aplikacije koju odabereš.

Podsjetnici. Isključeni su dok ih ne uključiš. Zakazuje ih tvoj telefon (Android WorkManager): najviše jedan na dan, ne kasno navečer i samo ako taj dan još nisi igrao. Sami podsjetnici ništa ne šalju. Isključuješ ih u Postavkama aplikacije ili u postavkama obavijesti Androida.

Sigurnosna kopija Androida. Ako je za tvoj Google račun uključeno sigurnosno kopiranje, ovi podaci mogu biti u kopiji (i u prijenosu na novi telefon), da novi telefon zadrži postavke, statistiku i igru u tijeku. Kopiju čuva Google u tvom računu; mi joj nemamo pristup.

Deinstalacija aplikacije briše te podatke s uređaja. Nemamo njihovu kopiju, pa ih ne možemo vratiti ni ikome predati.

Što napušta tvoj uređaj

Oglasi (Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, InMobi i Liftoff Monetize). Aplikacija prikazuje traku s oglasom na početnom zaslonu i u bela bloku; oglas u okviru označenom „Oglas” ispod rezultata dijeljenja, ispod svih gumba; oglas preko cijelog zaslona samo u stanci — nakon rezultata dijeljenja, na kraju partije ili kad započneš igru s početnog zaslona — nikad tijekom dijeljenja, najviše jednom u tri dijeljenja i ne češće od svake 3 minute; te kratki video samo kad ga sam odabereš: za vraćanje poteza, za propušteni izazov dana ili za novi pokušaj izazova dana. Da bi prikazao i mjerio oglase i spriječio prijevare, Googleov SDK za oglase (Google Mobile Ads) može automatski prikupljati i obrađivati:

  • identifikatore uređaja, npr. Androidov oglasni ID;
  • približnu lokaciju utvrđenu iz IP adrese;
  • interakcije s oglasima te dijagnostičke podatke, podatke o rušenju i podatke o radu;
  • signale Androidovih oglasnih sučelja (Privacy Sandbox: Topics, Attribution Reporting), gdje su dostupni.

Za neka oglasna mjesta mogu se natjecati i oglašivači mreža Unity Ads (Unity Technologies), Meta Audience Network (Meta Platforms), InMobi (InMobi Technology Services) i Liftoff Monetize (Liftoff Mobile), koje rade preko AdMoba. Njihovi SDK-ovi pokreću se tek nakon obrasca za pristanak, zajedno s Googleovim oglasima, i mogu prikupljati iste vrste podataka kao gore. Prema svojim pravilima privatnosti InMobi može prikupljati i podatke o mreži (operater, vrsta veze) i signale senzora uređaja (npr. akcelerometra i žiroskopa).

U zahtjevima za oglase ne šalju se nikakvi podaci o tvojim igrama.

Statistika korištenja (Google Analytics za Firebase). Da znamo kako se igra i što poboljšati, aplikacija šalje u Google Analytics za Firebase ove događaje: početak meča ili brze partije (s razinom računala, duljinom, regionalnim pravilima, brojem uključenih kućnih pravila i brojem partija u meču), početak i kraj svake partije (je li dobivena, kako je završila i koliko je dijeljenja imala), kraj meča, kraj prve odigrane partije (i je li to bilo na dan instalacije), kraj svakog dijeljenja (je li tvoj par zvao adut, je li zvač pao, štiglja, „dosta”, bela, je li djelitelj morao zvati, bodovi zvanja i koliko je dijeljenje trajalo), potez računala koji pravila nisu prihvatila (s fazom dijeljenja; služi samo za pronalaženje grešaka), početak i kraj izazova dana, novi pokušaj izazova dana (dodir gumba, početak — i je li prije bio video — i kraj), napredak u tjednom izazovu, videe koje odabereš (za što i kako su završili), zamolbu za ocjenu i dodir „Ocijeni igru na Google Playu”, dijeljenje rezultata kao slike, zaslone koje otvoriš, ponudu i dodire „Ukloni reklame” (i je li Trgovina Play bila dostupna te kako je kupnja završila) te jesu li reklame uklonjene. Firebase SDK sam dodaje podatke o sesijama, verziji aplikacije, uređaju i sustavu, približnu lokaciju utvrđenu iz IP adrese, slučajni identifikator ove instalacije aplikacije, Androidov oglasni ID (gdje to pristanak dopušta), odakle je aplikacija instalirana (preporuku instalacije iz Trgovine Play, npr. s koje kampanje ili dijeljenja) te kupnje u aplikaciji (proizvod i cijenu), a Googleov SDK za oglase — prikaze oglasa i njihovu procijenjenu vrijednost. Ne šaljemo karte, spremljene igre ni bilo kakav tekst. Gdje zakon traži pristanak, statistika se prikuplja tek nakon tvog odgovora u obrascu za pristanak i samo ako dopustiš pohranu podataka na uređaju.

Daljinske postavke (Firebase Remote Config). Uz isti pristanak aplikacija s Firebasea preuzima nekoliko postavki — npr. prikazuje li se oglas ispod rezultata dijeljenja — da bismo mogli usporediti dvije inačice igre na nasumično odabranim igračima. Pritom šalje Firebaseov identifikator instalacije, verziju aplikacije i sustava, jezik i zemlju. Nove postavke vrijede od sljedećeg pokretanja, nikad usred igre.

Kupnja „Ukloni reklame” (Google Play). Plaćanje u cijelosti obavlja Google Play. Ne primamo podatke o kartici ni o plaćanju — aplikacija od Google Playa dobiva samo potvrdu kupnje.

Ocjena na Google Playu. Aplikacija može prikazati Google Playev prozor za ocjenu, kojim u cijelosti upravlja Google.

Dijeljenje. „Podijeli pobjedu” i „Podijeli rezultat” izrađuju na tvom telefonu sliku tvog rezultata i predaju je — s jednim retkom teksta i poveznicom na Google Play — aplikaciji koju odabereš. Poveznica nosi oznaku (utm_source=share, vrstu slike i kampanju) da statistika Google Playa može prebrojati instalacije s dijeljenja; o tebi ne sadrži ništa.

E-pošta. Ako nam pišeš, primamo tvoju adresu e-pošte i poruku i koristimo ih samo za odgovor.

Što nikad ne prikupljamo

Mi (BoonWiz) ne primamo tvoje ime, adresu e-pošte (osim ako nam pišeš), kontakte, fotografije, datoteke ni točnu lokaciju. Osim Google Analyticsa za Firebase (gore) aplikacija ne koristi alate za analitiku ni za prijavu rušenja. Statistiku gledamo zbirno, u Googleovim izvješćima. Događaji kao što su prvo pokretanje ili završena partija mogu se koristiti i za mjerenje naših vlastitih oglasnih kampanja na Google Adsu.

Privacy Policy (English)

Bela – Croatian card game · com.boonwiz.bela

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Published by BoonWiz.

Summary

  • There is no account, sign-in or registration.
  • Your games, statistics, score sheet (bela blok) and settings stay on your phone. We have no server of our own.
  • With your consent where the law requires it, the app sends usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase — for example that a game started or ended — tied to a random ID for this installation, never to your name.
  • The app does not ask for access to your photos, files, contacts, camera or location. It asks to send notifications only if you turn reminders on.
  • The app is free because it shows Google AdMob ads. Ads can be removed with a one-time purchase.

What stays on your device

Stored in the app's internal storage. These records themselves are not sent anywhere (for usage statistics, see below):

  • Settings. The rules (the game's target, the number of games in a match, regional and house rules), the computer players' level, speed, the cards' look (mađarice or French, size, four colours), language and the reminder.
  • The game in progress. The match and the daily challenge in progress (cards, declarations, tricks played and the score), so you can finish them later.
  • Statistics. Matches and games played and won, deals, trump calls, contracts gone down, štiglje, declarations and belas, and for each player the points dealt into their hand and how often they held the jack of trumps.
  • Score sheet. The deals you enter.
  • Progress. Daily-challenge results, your daily streak, replays, the weekly challenge, your rank, rating and achievements.
  • Ads and questions. How many full-screen ads you have seen, when "Remove ads" was last offered, when we last asked you for a rating, and whether you have played your first game.
  • Reminders. Whether they are on and whether we have already asked if you want them, the hours of your last few visits (to remind you around the time you usually play), when you were last reminded, and how many reminders in a row went unopened.
  • Purchase. Whether "Remove ads" was bought.

Fair deals. The key from which every deal's cards are worked out is drawn on your device. Its code (a SHA-256 fingerprint) before the deal and the key itself after it are shown only to you, inside the app. They are not sent anywhere.

Daily challenge. The daily challenge's cards follow from the date alone: your phone works them out, with no server. Your result stays on the device; it leaves only if you share it yourself, through the app you pick.

Reminders. Off until you turn them on. Your phone schedules them (Android WorkManager): at most one a day, never late at night, and only if you have not played that day. The reminders themselves send nothing. Turn them off in the app's Settings or in Android's notification settings.

Android backup. If backup is turned on for your Google account, this data may be included in it (and in a transfer to a new phone), so a new phone keeps your settings, statistics and game in progress. Google stores that backup in your account; we have no access to it.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data from the device. We keep no copy, so we cannot restore it or hand it to anyone.

What leaves your device

Ads (Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, InMobi and Liftoff Monetize). The app shows a banner ad on the home screen and in the score sheet; an ad in a frame marked "Ad" under a deal's result, below every button; a full-screen ad only at a break — after a deal's result, at the end of a game, or when you start a game from the home screen — never during a deal, at most once every three deals and no more often than every 3 minutes; and a short video only when you choose one: to take back a move, to play a missed daily challenge, or for another go at the daily challenge. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ads SDK (Google Mobile Ads) may automatically collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID;
  • approximate location derived from the IP address;
  • ad interactions, diagnostics, crash and performance data;
  • signals from Android's advertising APIs (Privacy Sandbox: Topics, Attribution Reporting), where available.

Advertisers on Unity Ads (Unity Technologies), Meta Audience Network (Meta Platforms), InMobi (InMobi Technology Services) and Liftoff Monetize (Liftoff Mobile), working through AdMob, may also compete for some ad slots. Their SDKs start only after the consent form, together with Google's ads, and may collect the same kinds of data as above. According to its privacy policy, InMobi may also collect network information (carrier, connection type) and signals from the device's sensors (such as the accelerometer and gyroscope).

No data from your games is sent in ad requests.

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase). So that we know how the game is played and what to improve, the app sends these events to Google Analytics for Firebase: the start of a match or a quick game (with the computer players' level, the length, the regional rules, how many house rules are on and how many games the match is to), the start and end of every game (whether it was won, how it ended and how many deals it had), a match's end, the end of your first game (and whether it was on the day you installed the app), the end of every deal (whether your pair called trump, whether the caller went down, štiglja, "dosta", bela, whether the dealer had to call, the declarations' points and how long the deal took), a computer move the rules refused (with the stage of the deal; used only to find bugs), the daily challenge's start and end, another go at the daily challenge (the tap, its start — with whether a video came first — and its end), progress in the weekly challenge, the videos you choose (what for and how they ended), the request to rate the game and taps on "Rate the game on Google Play", sharing a result as a picture, the screens you open, the "Remove ads" offer and taps on it (with whether the Play Store was available and how the purchase ended), and whether ads are removed. The Firebase SDK adds session, app-version, device and system data, approximate location derived from the IP address, a random ID for this installation of the app, the Android advertising ID (where your consent allows it), where the app was installed from (the Play Store's install referrer, for example which campaign or share) and in-app purchases (product and price); Google's ads SDK adds ad impressions and their estimated value. We send no cards, saved games or text of any kind. Where the law requires consent, statistics are collected only after you answer the consent form, and only if you allow information to be stored on your device.

Remote switches (Firebase Remote Config). With the same consent, the app fetches a few switches from Firebase — for example whether to show the ad under a deal's result — so that we can compare two versions of the game on randomly chosen players. It sends a Firebase installation ID, the app and system version, language and country. New settings apply from the next launch, never during a game.

"Remove ads" purchase (Google Play). Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We receive no card or payment details — the app only gets a purchase confirmation from Google Play.

Google Play rating. The app may show the Google Play rating dialog, which Google handles entirely.

Sharing. "Share the win" and "Share the result" draw a picture of your result on your phone and hand it, with a line of text and a link to Google Play, to the app you pick. The link is tagged (utm_source=share, the kind of picture and a campaign) so that Google Play's statistics can count the installs sharing brings; it carries nothing about you.

Email. If you write to us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply.

What we never collect

We (BoonWiz) do not receive your name, email address (unless you write to us), contacts, photos, files or precise location. Apart from Google Analytics for Firebase (described above), the app uses no analytics or crash-reporting tools. We look at the statistics in aggregate, in Google's reports. Events such as a first launch or a finished game may also be used to measure our own ad campaigns on Google Ads.

Ads, statistics and your choices

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area — including Croatia — the UK and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form (UMP) before requesting ads and before collecting statistics. Your answer is stored on your device. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings.

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • The app works the same with personalised or non-personalised ads.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Retention

  • Data on the device: until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Ad data: retained by Google, Unity, Meta, InMobi and Liftoff under their own policies.
  • Usage statistics: Google Analytics keeps individual events for at most 14 months, and data tied to the installation ID for at most 14 months after its last activity; after that, only aggregate reports remain.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability and objection. Because game data exists only on your device, you can exercise these rights yourself straight away: reset statistics in Statistics → Reset statistics, start a new score sheet in Score sheet → New sheet, clear data in Android Settings → Apps → Bela → Storage, or uninstall the app. For ad data and statistics, use the consent settings in the app or Google's ad settings on your device. Statistics carry no name or contact details: they are tied to a random installation ID (and, where your consent allows, the advertising ID). Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it starts a new installation ID. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority — in Croatia, the Personal Data Protection Agency (Agencija za zaštitu osobnih podataka, AZOP, azop.hr).

For anything else, write to boonyboonapp@gmail.com. We do not sell personal data.
