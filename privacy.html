Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-10

Applies to: Video Compressor: Resize MB · Image Compressor: Photo to KB · PDF Compressor: Reduce to KB · Audio Converter & MP3 Cutter (“the Apps”), published by Boonwiz (“we”).

The short version

Your files are processed entirely on your device — we never receive, store, or see your videos, photos, documents, or audio. The Apps need no account. The free version is supported by short ads — reward ads you choose to watch and, in some regions, a brief ad while a longer job processes; ads are the only reason any advertising data exists in these Apps, and going Pro removes ads entirely.

1. Your content

All compression, conversion, and editing happens locally on your device. Your files are never uploaded to us or anyone else. Temporary working copies stay on your device and are deleted within 24 hours or when you delete them. Exported images have location (EXIF) metadata removed.

2. Information collected

(a) Advertising data (free version only). We use Google AdMob to show ads in the free version — optional reward ads and, in some regions, a brief ad while a longer job processes. When an ad is served, Google receives your device's advertising ID and technical data (device model, IP-derived coarse location, ad interaction) under Google's own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). In the EEA/UK/Switzerland we ask for your consent first via Google's consent form; if you decline, ads are non-personalized. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Pro removes ads and this data flow entirely.

(b) Purchase information. Purchases are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions; it receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and your purchase history for these Apps so Pro works and can be restored. We never receive your name, email, or card details.

(c) Anonymous usage analytics. TelemetryDeck counts anonymized events (e.g., “a file was compressed”) to help us improve the Apps. Aggregated; cannot identify you.

(d) Support email. If you write to boonyboonapp@gmail.com, we use what you send only to help you.

3. What we don't do

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your files for anything, including AI training. No accounts, no contact lists, no precise location, no banner or pop-up ads.

4. Legal bases & your rights (GDPR/UK GDPR/CCPA and similar)

Ads (EEA/UK): consent, withdrawable anytime via the in-app privacy options link in Settings. Purchases: contract. Analytics: legitimate interest, anonymous. You may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object; contact boonyboonapp@gmail.com (response within 30 days). California residents: we do not “sell” personal information; ad personalization can be limited via consent choices and Android's ad settings.

5. Retention

Your files: on your device, yours. Analytics: aggregate. Purchase records: as required by tax law. Support email: ≤24 months. Advertising data: held by Google per its policy, not by us.

6. Children

The Apps are not directed at children under 13; ads are configured as not child-directed with G-rated content only.

7. Security

Billing, ads, and analytics traffic is TLS-encrypted. Your files never transit at all.

8. Service providers

Google LLC (Play billing, AdMob ads), RevenueCat Inc. (subscriptions), TelemetryDeck (anonymous analytics).
