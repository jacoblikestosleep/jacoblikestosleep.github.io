Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-15

Applies to: Video Compressor: Resize MB · Image Compressor: Photo to KB · PDF Compressor: Reduce to KB · Audio Converter & MP3 Cutter · Sweep: Duplicate Photo Cleaner (“the Apps”), published by Boonwiz (“we”).

The short version

Your files are processed entirely on your device — we never receive, store, or see your videos, photos, documents, or audio. The Apps need no account. The free version is supported by short ads — reward ads you choose to watch and, in some regions, a brief ad while a longer job processes; ads are the only reason any advertising data exists in these Apps, and going Pro removes ads entirely.

1. Your content

All compression, conversion, editing, and scanning happens locally on your device. Your files are never uploaded to us or anyone else. Temporary working copies stay on your device and are deleted within 24 hours or when you delete them. Exported images have location (EXIF) metadata removed.

1a. Media library access

Sweep: Duplicate Photo Cleaner asks for permission to read your photos, videos, and audio (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, and on Android 12 and below READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE). It needs this because finding duplicates means comparing files against one another across your whole library — you cannot pick out duplicates you do not yet know exist.

Sweep reads your files to compare them, and reads file size, date, and folder name to find large, old, or leftover files. Nothing it reads is sent anywhere. Deleting is always done through Android's own confirmation dialog, and anything you delete is kept in Sweep's recycle bin for 7 days so you can restore it.

Video Compressor: Resize MB asks for permission to read videos (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO) for one optional feature: telling you when you have recorded a video too large to share. It reads only each video's size, date, and folder — never the video itself. Declining changes nothing else about the app.

The other three Apps request no media permission at all. They use Android's built-in photo picker, which hands them only the files you choose.

2. Information collected

(a) Advertising data (free version only). We use Google AdMob to show ads in the free version — optional reward ads and, in some regions, a brief ad while a longer job processes. When an ad is served, Google receives your device's advertising ID and technical data (device model, IP-derived coarse location, ad interaction) under Google's own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). In the EEA/UK/Switzerland we ask for your consent first via Google's consent form; if you decline, ads are non-personalized. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Pro removes ads and this data flow entirely.

(b) Purchase information. Purchases are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions; it receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and your purchase history for these Apps so Pro works and can be restored. We never receive your name, email, or card details.

(c) Usage analytics: none. The Apps contain no analytics SDK and send us no usage data of any kind. We do not know how often you use them, which features you use, or whether a job succeeded. Earlier versions included anonymous analytics; it was removed.

(d) Support email. If you write to boonyboonapp@gmail.com, we use what you send only to help you.

3. What we don't do

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your files for anything, including AI training. No accounts, no contact lists, no precise location, no banner or pop-up ads.
