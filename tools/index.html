On-device is the whole point

Most free compressors upload your file, process it on a server and hand it back. That is someone else holding your video.

  • ✓Nothing is uploaded. Compression, conversion, editing and scanning all happen locally. We never receive, store or see your files.
  • ✓Working copies are temporary. They stay on your device and are deleted within 24 hours, or the moment you delete them.
  • ✓Location data is stripped. Exported images have their EXIF location metadata removed, so a photo you send does not carry where it was taken.
  • ✓No account. Nothing to register, no email to hand over, nothing to leak.
  • ✓Your files stay yours. We claim no rights over anything you process — and as the privacy policy sets out, we never receive it in the first place.
